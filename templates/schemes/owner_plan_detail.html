Retirement preserves agreements

Retirement stops new applications and enrolments and removes the offer from public lists. It does not cancel customers' agreements, remove balances or change their accepted payment and settlement rights.

Review pending applications before retiring. They remain in history and cannot be converted using a retired offer; changed terms need a separately reviewed offer. Retirement is permanent; create a separate offer for future use.
