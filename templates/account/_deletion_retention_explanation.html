What is removed and what may remain

After verification and review, completion removes your login credentials, connected Google sign-in, sessions, invitations and unnecessary profile details. This removes access to this customer account, not your Google account.

We retain only the identity and contact details needed for an outstanding entitlement or another documented obligation. Necessary scheme agreements, contributions, allocations, receipts, settlements and audit evidence may remain for accounting, tax, dispute or other justified purposes. Deletion does not forfeit your savings or accumulated metal.

The outcome notice explains the records retained, why they remain, the retention period or release condition, and the next review date. A review date is not an automatic deletion date. Minimal privacy-request and consent evidence and protected internal identifiers may also remain; we do not claim that all historical records become anonymous.

Provider-held records, support correspondence, logs, exported or paper copies and backups are reviewed separately. They are not automatically erased by removing your login. Backup copies may remain until their retention cycle expires and must not be used to reactivate a removed account or restore erased contact details.

The email address needed to send your outcome notice is temporarily held in its delivery queue and cleared after our email provider accepts the message. Provider acceptance does not prove inbox delivery. For statements, settlement or questions about retained data, contact admin@jaishrikrishnajewellery.com.
