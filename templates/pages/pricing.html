Getting started

How to join and contribute

Enrolment is completed at the showroom after you review and accept the applicable terms. The store then creates your customer login. Online payment is available only after enrolment and only for amounts allowed by your recorded plan.

The current Jai Shri Krishna Jewellery Scheme Rate for your selected metal is shown and locked before payment starts. After verification, your INR contribution is converted to a recorded gold or silver quantity using that rate. A later Scheme Rate does not change quantities already credited.

Once the plan becomes eligible, the accumulated metal quantity may be applied toward jewellery or an eligible matching-metal coin at our showroom. It cannot be withdrawn as cash through this website.
