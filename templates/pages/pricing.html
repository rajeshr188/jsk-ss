Before you apply

Applying is a non-binding request. It does not create a savings agreement.

  • The showroom verifies your details and confirms the current terms before creating your agreement. Payment starts after enrolment.
  • Each verified payment credits your enrolled metal grade at the rate locked before payment.
  • Once eligible, use your accumulated metal toward jewellery or an eligible matching-metal coin at our showroom. This plan does not provide voluntary cash withdrawals.
  • Making charges, wastage/value addition and taxes are confirmed at purchase; they are not included in the displayed contribution amount unless expressly stated in your terms.
