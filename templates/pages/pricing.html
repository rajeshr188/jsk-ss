Getting started

How to join and contribute

Enrolment is completed at the showroom after you review and accept the applicable terms. The store then creates your customer login. Online payment is available only after enrolment and only for amounts allowed by your recorded plan.

For gold or silver modes, the current Jai Shri Krishna Jewellery Scheme Rate is shown and locked before payment starts. A verified INR contribution is converted using that locked rate, and the final allocated quantity is shown after verification. A later rate publication does not change that contribution.
