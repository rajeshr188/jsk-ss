About us

About Jai Shri Krishna Jewellery

We are a jewellery business based in Thorapadi, Vellore, offering owner-managed savings schemes that help customers plan eligible jewellery or coin purchases over time.

Our savings schemes

Customers may be enrolled in cash, gold, or silver savings modes. Each enrolment records its contribution amount or range, frequency, duration, eligibility date, and any applicable cash bonus terms. Gold and silver contributions use the Jai Shri Krishna Jewellery Scheme Rate shown and locked before that payment begins.

The online service lets enrolled customers make contributions, review their history and balances, and access acknowledgements and statements. It does not operate as a public deposit account, investment-trading service, or online jewellery inventory.

How enrolment works

  1. Review the currently available plans and pricing.
  2. Contact or visit our showroom to discuss the suitable plan and savings mode.
  3. After the terms are agreed, the store creates the customer login and scheme enrolment.
  4. The enrolled customer signs in to make eligible contributions and track the scheme.

Public self-registration is not currently offered. A login alone does not create a scheme agreement.
