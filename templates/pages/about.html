About us

About Jai Shri Krishna Jewellery

We are a jewellery business based in Thorapadi, Vellore, offering owner-managed gold and silver plans that help customers work toward an eligible showroom jewellery or coin purchase over time.

Our savings schemes

Customers choose gold or silver when they enrol. Each enrolment records its INR contribution amount or range, frequency, duration, and eligibility date. Before each payment, the applicable Jai Shri Krishna Jewellery Scheme Rate is shown and locked; after verification, the contribution is converted to a recorded quantity of the selected metal.

At eligibility, the accumulated metal quantity may be applied toward eligible jewellery or a matching-metal coin at our showroom. Product selection, final pricing, taxes, making charges, wastage/value addition, invoicing, and collection are completed at the showroom.

The online service lets enrolled customers make contributions, review their history and metal quantities, and access acknowledgements and statements. It is not a bank deposit, an interest-bearing account, a metal-trading service, or an online jewellery inventory.

How enrolment works

  1. Review the currently available savings plans.
  2. Contact or visit our showroom to discuss the suitable plan and metal choice.
  3. After the terms are agreed, the store creates the customer login and scheme enrolment.
  4. The enrolled customer signs in to make eligible contributions and track the scheme.

Public self-registration is not currently offered. A login alone does not create a scheme agreement.
