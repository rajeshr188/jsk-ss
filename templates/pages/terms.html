4. Metal grams and INR purchase credit

Each exact-metal enrolment is tied to an exact gold or silver grade, and liabilities for different grades remain separate. Before a payment begins, the website shows and locks the applicable Jai Sri Krishna Jewellery Scheme Rate for that grade. After verified payment, the INR amount is converted to a quantity of the enrolled grade using that locked rate. A later Scheme Rate publication does not change a quantity already credited.

The credited quantity is a plan entitlement recorded for later redemption; it does not mean that a specific jewellery item has been selected, priced, purchased, or delivered at the time of contribution. Scheme Rates and jewellery prices can rise or fall. The plan is not a bank deposit, an interest-bearing account, an investment or metal-trading product, or a cash-withdrawal service.

The preceding rate and quantity rules apply to exact-metal agreements. INR jewellery purchase-credit agreements, when offered, retain verified paid principal in INR. No metal rate is locked and no grams are bought. Any expressly included purchase-only benefit remains separate from principal.
