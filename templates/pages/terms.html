Terms and conditions

These terms govern use of the Jai Sri Krishna Jewellery website and participation in the gold and silver jewellery purchase plans publicly described on this website. Jai Sri Krishna Jewellery operates from No. 155, Azad Road, Thorapadi, Vellore, Tamil Nadu 632001, India.

1. Enrolment

Public self-registration is not available. A customer becomes enrolled only after the showroom creates a customer profile and a specific scheme account. The account records the selected plan, gold or silver metal choice, contribution rules, duration, and eligibility date. A website login by itself is not an enrolment or agreement.

2. Plans and contributions

Current savings plans display their contribution amount or range, frequency, and duration on the savings plans page. Contributions are paid in INR. Fixed plans accept only the recorded fixed amount; variable plans accept amounts within their recorded range. Once-per-month plans permit only one successful contribution in a calendar month. Contributions are not accepted outside the dates allowed by the enrolled terms.

3. Online payments

Online payments are processed through Razorpay. A browser success message alone does not credit a scheme. The payment must be verified and captured by the payment provider. Pending or failed attempts create no savings entitlement. Customers should retain the payment reference and contribution acknowledgement.

4. Gold and silver quantity records

Each enrolment is tied to an exact gold or silver grade, and liabilities for different grades remain separate. Before a payment begins, the website shows and locks the applicable Jai Sri Krishna Jewellery Scheme Rate for that grade. After verified payment, the INR amount is converted to a quantity of the enrolled grade using that locked rate. A later Scheme Rate publication does not change a quantity already credited.

The credited quantity is a plan entitlement recorded for later redemption; it does not mean that a specific jewellery item has been selected, priced, purchased, or delivered at the time of contribution. Scheme Rates and jewellery prices can rise or fall. The plan is not a bank deposit, an interest-bearing account, an investment or metal-trading product, or a cash-withdrawal service.

5. Duration and eligibility

The minimum scheme duration is shown at enrolment and is at least 12 months. Reaching the eligibility date makes the accumulated metal quantity eligible for redemption; it does not automatically purchase jewellery or close the account.

6. Early discontinuation

An early-discontinuation request must be made within 12 months of the scheme start date. Paid contributions are not voluntarily returned as cash merely because the customer chooses to stop early. The eligible accumulated metal quantity may instead be applied at our showroom toward jewellery or an eligible matching-metal coin.

  • Discontinuation during the first 0–5 completed months receives no scheme benefit or discount on wastage/value addition.
  • From 6 completed months until maturity, any partial wastage/value-addition discount is limited to the schedule stated in the customer’s written enrolment terms. If no such schedule is recorded, no additional discount is promised by this website.

7. Redemption and purchases

At eligibility, an accumulated gold or silver quantity may be settled in the matching metal or applied toward an eligible jewellery purchase under the recorded enrolment terms. These plans are not converted to cash by this application. Showroom purchases are subject to product availability, prevailing product price, applicable taxes, making charges, wastage/value addition, and invoice terms. Showroom staff complete the selection, invoicing, and redemption record; the website does not dispatch products or execute payouts automatically.

8. Payment errors, refunds, and disputes

Duplicate, erroneous, or captured payments that create no corresponding entitlement may qualify for a source refund. Customers must report such a payment within 24 hours. See the Cancellation and Refund Policy for investigation and processing timelines. This does not limit rights available under applicable law or card/payment-network rules.

9. Account security and acceptable use

Customers must provide accurate information, keep login credentials confidential, and promptly report suspected unauthorised access or payment activity. The website must not be used unlawfully, to interfere with service operation, or to attempt access to another customer’s records.

10. Availability and corrections

We may temporarily suspend the website for security, maintenance, or provider outages. Financial records are corrected through traceable reversal or reconciliation procedures rather than hidden deletion. These public terms apply to the gold and silver plans described here; an existing customer's valid recorded enrolment terms continue to govern that account. If website information conflicts with those terms, contact the showroom so the discrepancy can be reviewed.
