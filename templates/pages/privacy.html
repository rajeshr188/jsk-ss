Privacy policy

Jai Sri Krishna Jewellery respects customer privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information through our showroom, savings-scheme website and supporting Android app.

Information we collect

  • Identity and contact details, including name, email address, mobile number, and address supplied in a customer-access request or at the showroom.
  • Account and enrolment information, including customer and scheme numbers, selected plan, savings mode, dates, and agreed terms.
  • Transaction records, including contribution amounts, payment-provider references, payment status, locked Scheme Rates and metal allocations, redemptions, reversals, receipts, and statements.
  • Support communications and information supplied when reporting a payment or account issue.
  • Technical and security information such as login/session data, request logs, timestamps, error records, and one-way digests used to limit repeated registration requests without retaining another copy of the submitted identifier.
  • If you explicitly connect Google sign-in, Google's stable account identifier, verified email assertion, and basic profile metadata returned by Google. We do not retain Google OAuth access or refresh tokens.

Payment credentials such as full card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, or online-banking passwords are entered into Razorpay’s payment interface and are not stored by this website.

How we use information

Optional Google sign-in is used only to authenticate an already-approved customer who explicitly connected it. It does not register or approve a customer and does not create a scheme.

We use personal information to verify and review customer-access requests, create and secure approved customer accounts, record agreed enrolments, validate contributions, allocate metal, calculate eligibility and balances, process and document redemptions, provide support, investigate errors, prevent fraud, maintain audit records, and comply with legal, accounting, tax, and payment-network obligations. A registration request may be approved or rejected by the showroom and does not itself create a scheme.

Sharing and service providers

When you choose Google sign-in, Google processes the authentication request under its own terms and returns the identity information described above.

We share information only as reasonably necessary with payment processors such as Razorpay, banking partners, hosting and database providers, email-delivery providers, professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, or other parties where required by law. Service providers receive only the information needed for their function. We do not sell customer personal information.

Cookies and sessions

The website uses essential cookies for login sessions, request security, and account functionality. These are not used by us for third-party advertising. Razorpay may process information under its own privacy terms when its checkout is opened.

Retention

We retain necessary scheme and financial evidence while needed to establish entitlements and meet accounting, tax, dispute or other applicable obligations. This does not require keeping every profile field or unrelated message indefinitely. For records covered by section 36 of the CGST Act, the retention period is 72 months from the due date for the relevant annual return, with extensions for applicable proceedings or investigations. Other applicable obligations may require longer retention; this is not a six-year period starting from account deletion.

Registration rate-limit digests have a configured short retention window; cleanup runs when further requests are processed, so expiry is not a guarantee of immediate physical deletion. Registration decisions, consent and privacy-request evidence require their own documented purposes and reviews. Security logs and support records are reviewed separately from statutory transaction evidence. We do not automatically destroy financial history when a review date arrives.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted HTTPS transport, restricted production systems, payment-signature verification, and traceable financial records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Customers should use a strong password and immediately report suspected account misuse.

Your choices and requests

You may ask about a connected Google identity. Online unlink is not available in the initial release; contact the showroom immediately so a suspected compromised connection can be disabled and reviewed.

You may ask us to review or correct your contact information and may raise questions about access, retention, or lawful processing. Some information cannot be removed while retention is required for financial, contractual, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal purposes. We will respond according to applicable law after verifying the requester’s identity.
