What to keep in mind

  • Contributions do not reserve a jewellery item, guarantee future prices or create investment returns.
  • Making charges, wastage/value addition and taxes are confirmed on the purchase invoice. A benefit applies only if expressly recorded and qualified under your agreement.
  • Showroom-only selection, invoicing and fulfilment. No online jewellery delivery or automatic cash withdrawal.
  • Pending, duplicate or uncertain payments need reconciliation. Do not pay again merely because confirmation is delayed.
  • Later offer edits do not rewrite an existing agreement. Its accepted rules and retained financial records remain authoritative.
