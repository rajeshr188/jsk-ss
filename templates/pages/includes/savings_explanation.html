Our savings schemes

Each listed offer explains what accumulates, contribution rules, eligibility and any conditional benefit. Exact-metal agreements record one agreed gold or silver grade using the rate locked for verified payments. INR purchase-credit agreements, when offered, retain verified paid principal in rupees with no metal rate or grams.

When eligible, accumulated metal quantity may be applied toward the agreed jewellery fulfilment; INR purchase credit is applied toward the invoice with any unused paid principal handled through the original-source final-refund procedure. Configured agreements use one final full settlement. Product selection, final pricing, taxes, making/value-addition charges and collection are confirmed at the showroom.

Customers can review verified contributions, their recorded entitlement, receipts and statements. This is not a bank deposit, an interest-bearing investment, a metal-trading service or a general cash-withdrawal account. Existing agreements retain their accepted rights.

How enrolment works

  1. Compare currently available savings plans and their worked examples.
  2. Apply through the offered online route or contact the showroom for help. Verify your email and confirm the selected terms.
  3. The showroom reviews the application and creates an agreement only after confirmation. A login alone does not create a scheme agreement.
  4. After enrolment, sign in to make eligible contributions and follow your records.
