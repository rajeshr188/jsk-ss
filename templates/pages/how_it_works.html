Before enrolment

An online request is the beginning—not the agreement.

Customer access

Submit your contact details, verify your email, and wait for owner approval. Approval creates a secure customer login only.

Plan request

An approved customer may request a displayed plan and metal grade. The request is non-binding and creates no payment access.

Showroom confirmation

We contact you, review the current terms, and separately create the savings agreement only after confirmation.

After enrolment

Each verified contribution becomes a traceable record.

Your account keeps the contribution history understandable without turning it into a metal-trading service.

When your plan is eligible

Select your jewellery at the showroom.

Your eligible accumulated metal quantity may be applied toward jewellery or an eligible coin of the matching metal under your recorded terms. Jewellery selection, final product price, applicable taxes, making charges, and wastage or value addition are confirmed at the showroom.

A contribution does not reserve or purchase a specific jewellery item. The website does not provide withdrawals or metal trading.
