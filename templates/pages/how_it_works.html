Before enrolment

An online request is the beginning—not the agreement.

Customer access

Verify your email and complete your details. A login alone is not an agreement. A plan-first application can coordinate access and enrolment after showroom approval.

Plan request

Choose a displayed offer and, for metal plans, one offered grade. Existing customers can request another plan. Submission is non-binding and creates no payment access.

Showroom confirmation

We contact you, review the current terms, and separately create the savings agreement only after confirmation.

After enrolment

Each verified contribution becomes a traceable record.

Your agreement specifies whether contributions record exact-grade grams or INR jewellery purchase credit. These are separate entitlements, never a combined cash balance.

₹
Pay in INRContribute only the amount and frequency allowed by your recorded plan.
Metal: see the rate firstOnly metal agreements use the rate locked for their enrolled grade before payment.
INR credit: retain rupeesVerified purchase-credit principal stays in INR. It does not buy grams or lock a metal rate.
Keep the evidenceReview payment history, receipts and statements; metal agreements also show locked rates and quantities.

When your plan is eligible

Select your jewellery at the showroom.

Metal agreements apply the agreed exact-grade quantity toward eligible jewellery under their recorded terms. INR purchase-credit agreements apply verified paid principal toward the invoice; any unused paid principal is handled within the single final settlement through the original-source refund procedure. Purchase-only benefits are separate and cannot be refunded as cash.

Configured agreements require one final full settlement with customer authorization, reconciliation and evidence of actual fulfilment. A date alone is not always enough: some offers require a qualifying payment count, or both duration and count. See your specific offer.

A contribution does not reserve or purchase a specific jewellery item. Final prices, taxes and making/value-addition charges are confirmed at the showroom. There is no automatic cash-withdrawal or metal-trading facility.
