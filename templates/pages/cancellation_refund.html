Cancellation and refund policy

This policy distinguishes exact-metal settlement, final settlement of INR jewellery purchase credit, and correction of duplicate, erroneous, unauthorised or unmatched payments. The accepted agreement determines which arrangement applies. Later website wording does not rewrite existing agreements.

Voluntary early discontinuation

Early discontinuation means requesting to end an agreement before its normal eligibility conditions are met, including agreements longer than twelve months. Contact the showroom to review your accepted terms and authorize a supported settlement. Choosing to discontinue an exact-metal agreement does not convert its grams into a voluntary cash refund. Recorded eligible metal is applied toward the agreed showroom fulfilment.

  • Benefits: only an expressly recorded and qualified benefit applies. There is no general 0–5/6-month discount schedule; any historical written schedule remains specific to that agreement.
  • No early-cancellation fee: the recorded eligible metal quantity itself is not reduced as an early-cancellation fee. An unearned benefit or discount is not part of that quantity.

Early discontinuation must be arranged with the showroom; it is not performed automatically by closing the browser account.

Configured INR purchase-credit agreements

INR purchase credit records verified paid principal, not gold or silver grams. One final full settlement records the principal used for a jewellery invoice and any unused paid principal returned to its original source. This permits a final unused-principal refund; it does not provide repeated partial withdrawals, cashable benefits or a replacement-credit voucher.

The showroom records invoice allocation, refund source/reference and actual fulfilment evidence. The agreement is not completed while principal remains outstanding or payment/refund issues are unresolved. A purchase-only bonus or discount is separate from paid principal and cannot be refunded as cash. An unused configured agreement can be cancelled through the recorded process without a financial settlement.

How original-source refunds are handled

Razorpay-origin payments: an approved refund is initiated manually through the Razorpay dashboard to the original payment method. These payments are not returned as physical showroom cash. Showroom cash-origin payments: an approved cash return is fulfilled in person with retained receipt and customer acknowledgement. Mixed sources are reconciled separately; the app does not automatically execute a refund.

Eligible metal agreements

Voluntary early-discontinuation restrictions do not remove the recorded maturity terms. Once eligible, the accumulated gold or silver quantity may be settled in the matching metal or applied toward an eligible jewellery purchase at the showroom. It is not converted to cash by this application. The exact metal entitlement is determined from the recorded enrolment terms and verified contribution history.

Duplicate or erroneous captured payments

A duplicate payment, a captured payment made for the wrong eligible account or amount, or a captured payment that creates no corresponding entitlement may qualify for correction and a refund to the original payment method.

  1. Report the issue within 24 hours of payment by email, phone, or showroom visit.
  2. Provide the customer/scheme number, amount, date, and Razorpay payment reference. Never share a card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, or banking password.
  3. We will investigate and, when approved, initiate the source refund within 48 hours after receiving sufficient information.
  4. No deduction or refund fee is charged by Jai Sri Krishna Jewellery for an approved payment-error refund.

After initiation, the bank or payment provider may take up to 7 working days to credit the original payment method. Provider or bank delays are outside our direct control, but we will provide the available refund reference for tracking.

Failed or pending payments

A failed or pending payment does not create a scheme entitlement. If money is debited but the payment is not captured, the bank or payment provider may reverse it automatically. Report the issue if the debit is not reversed within the period shown by your bank or provider.

Unauthorised transactions and disputes

Contact us immediately and notify your bank or payment provider if you believe a transaction was unauthorised. Chargebacks and payment-network disputes follow the issuing bank’s and Razorpay’s procedures. Nothing in this policy limits rights available under applicable law.
