Shipping and delivery policy

Showroom pickup only

Jai Sri Krishna Jewelley does not currently ship or deliver jewellery, coins, or metal redeemed through these plans. Jewellery selection, invoicing, physical fulfilment, and redemption collection take place in person at our showroom:

No. 155, Azad Road
Thorapadi, Vellore
Tamil Nadu 632001, India

Because delivery is not offered, there is no delivery area, shipping charge, or courier-delivery timeline.

Arranging collection

Contact the showroom before visiting to confirm eligibility, product or coin availability, applicable pricing and charges, required documentation, and the expected collection time. Custom or unavailable items may require a separately agreed preparation period before pickup.

Verification at pickup

The customer may be asked to provide the scheme number, registered contact information, suitable identification, and any related invoice or redemption reference. Customers should inspect physical items and verify the invoice at handover. Fulfilment is recorded only after the showroom completes the redemption or purchase process.

Online account updates

The customer’s online scheme account reflects verified contributions and recorded redemptions; it is not a shipment tracker. Payment verification or metal allocation can occasionally remain pending while an operational exception is investigated. A pending status does not mean that an item has been dispatched.

Contact

For pickup arrangements, email admin@jaishrikrishnajewellery.com or call +91 94894 81436. Showroom hours are Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–9:00 PM, and Sunday, 9:00 AM–1:00 PM, subject to public-holiday changes.
